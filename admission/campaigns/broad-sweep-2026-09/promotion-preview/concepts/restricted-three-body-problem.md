The circular restricted three-body problem is an autonomous rotating-frame dynamical model for a massless body moving under two circularly orbiting primaries.

## Mathematical skeleton

A four-dimensional first-order system combines Coriolis terms with the gradient of an effective potential.

The claim is scoped to Planar circular restricted problem. Its stated validity regime is: Newtonian point-mass gravity in the rotating frame.

## Boundaries

- Spatial, elliptic, relativistic, and finite-mass variants change the model.

Counterexamples and failure probes:

- A third body with appreciable mass invalidates the restricted assumption.
