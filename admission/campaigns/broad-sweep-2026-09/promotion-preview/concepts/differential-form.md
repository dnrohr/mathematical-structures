Differential forms provide the coordinate-independent representation that unifies gradient, circulation, flux, and integral theorems from vector calculus.

## Mathematical skeleton

The exterior derivative and pullback combine with Stokes theorem integral over boundary equals integral of the derivative.

The claim is scoped to Smooth manifolds and classical vector calculus under metric-dependent identifications. Its stated validity regime is: Differential forms of compatible degree on manifolds with boundary.

## Boundaries

- Gradient and curl identifications use a metric and orientation in addition to forms.

Counterexamples and failure probes:

- Nonorientable manifolds require densities or twisted forms for global integration.

## Adversarial review

**Challenge.** Saying forms are identical to vector calculus hides metric-dependent conversions.

**Response.** The claim says representation and explicitly separates metric identifications.
