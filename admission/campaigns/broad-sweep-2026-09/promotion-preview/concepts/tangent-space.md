The tangent space is the linear local representation in which first-order linearization of smooth manifold dynamics lives.

## Mathematical skeleton

The derivative Df_p is a linear map from T_pM to T_f(p)N.

The claim is scoped to Smooth finite-dimensional manifolds. Its stated validity regime is: First-order local approximation.

## Boundaries

- A tangent space is not globally identical to the manifold.

Counterexamples and failure probes:

- Singular spaces can lack a constant-dimensional manifold tangent bundle.

## Adversarial review

**Challenge.** Visual tangent planes encourage an embedding-dependent definition.

**Response.** The claim uses the intrinsic derivative construction.
