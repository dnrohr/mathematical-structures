The minimax theorem equates maximin and minimax values in finite two-player zero-sum games with mixed strategies.

## Mathematical skeleton

Convexification by probability simplices permits interchange of max and min.

The claim is scoped to Finite two-player zero-sum games. Its stated validity regime is: The stated claim is limited to finite two-player zero-sum games.

## Boundaries

- The equality generally fails outside zero-sum or suitable convex-concave settings.

Counterexamples and failure probes:

- A general-sum bimatrix game has no single opposing value to equate.
