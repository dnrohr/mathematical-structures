# Bridge Atlas curatorial review

Reviewed against the schema 1.6.0 artifact generated on 2026-09-29. This is a
meaning audit, not a claim that the community partition is an ontology.

## Five busiest crossings

1. **Communities 2–6 (9 claims).** The aggregate is driven by spectral tools,
   linearization, feedback and stability applications. It is coherent as a
   migration crossing, but the individual claims mix representation,
   governance and application relationships; the neutral aggregate line is
   therefore necessary.
2. **Communities 6–7 (6 claims).** The claims connect spectral/probabilistic
   machinery with conservation, phase-space and symmetry structures. Both
   theorem and special-case strengths occur, confirming that one aggregate
   strength would overstate the data.
3. **Communities 2–7 (6 claims).** Bifurcation, linearization and stability
   connect to phase space and chaos, while application claims reach motor and
   structural systems. Direction remains meaningful only in the claim list.
4. **Communities 1–6 (5 claims).** Fourier, diffusion, Green's functions,
   eigenvalues and large-number limits form a recognizable analytical bridge.
   The five exact claims are more informative than a field-level label.
5. **Communities 4–6 (4 claims).** Hidden/state-space models connect to Markov
   chains, Bayes' rule and eigenvalue representations. The aggregate is
   coherent without implying that every member of either community is linked.

Every indexed edge in these aggregates was checked by the automated direct
trusted-graph scan. The overview has no directional marker and discloses the
stored direction, type, strength, context and evidence for each claim.

## Frontiers

The complete zero-count set is `0–2`, `0–3`, `0–7`, `3–4`, and `4–7`.
The artifact complement test confirms there is no trusted crossing for these
pairs. The interface labels them “No trusted edge recorded in this dataset,”
states that absence is not evidence of unrelatedness, and provides no action
that fabricates endpoints or pre-fills a proposed edge. When a filter hides a
globally populated crossing, the wording changes to “No visible trusted edge
under these filters.”

## Landmark bundles

The eight bundles are generated by trusted degree, then betweenness, then
slug. They are recognizable descriptions of their partitions—for example
Markov chains · Eigenvalues · Diffusion and Phase space · Conservation laws ·
Symmetry—but remain explicitly descriptive rather than editorial field names.
The complete member lists remain in the artifact and the accessible community
list.

## Cross-view parity

Bridge counts and indexes are verified against a direct scan of the same
trusted edges used by the adjacency matrix. A selected known bridge links to
the matrix with one exact representative endpoint pair selected; every claim
remains independently auditable there. The focused Atlas handoff uses the
existing `focus=<slug>&depth=1` model rather than creating a second concept
focus state.
